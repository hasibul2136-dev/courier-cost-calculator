# AMAN GRAPHICS AND DESIGNS LTD. - Dynamic Courier Cost Calculator

A dynamic, real-time courier price calculation and comparison dashboard designed to replace outdated static Excel sheets. Tailored for commercial, merchandising, design, and sampling teams generating **Work Orders (WO)**, shipping instructions, and **Purchase Orders**.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Desktop Shortcut:**
   - Double-click `Launch Courier Calculator.bat` on your **Desktop** (`D:\Office\OneDrive - Aman Knittings Ltd\Desktop\Launch Courier Calculator.bat`).
2. **From the Project Directory:**
   - Double-click `start_calculator.bat` inside `D:\AG Projects\Courier Cost Calculator\`.

The tool runs a local server and opens your default browser automatically at `http://127.0.0.1:5050`.

## 📦 What's New & Included

1. **Company Branding:**
   - Updated for **AMAN GRAPHICS AND DESIGNS LTD.** across all UI elements, Work Order exports, and Excel sheets.
2. **From & To Location Support:**
   - **Origin (From):** Fast selection of Dhaka (Head Office), Gazipur (Factory), Narayanganj, Chattogram, or custom location.
   - **Destination (To):** 1-click destination buttons for **China (🇨🇳)**, **Hong Kong (🇭🇰)**, **USA (🇺🇸)**, **UK (🇬🇧)**, **Germany (🇩🇪)**, **Spain (🇪🇸)**, **Italy (🇮🇹)**, **India (🇮🇳)**, **Turkey (🇹🇷)**, and other worldwide hubs.
3. **Zone-Specific Pricing:**
   - Automatically applies the correct tariff zone (e.g. Far East / China & HK, USA & North America, Europe & UK, South Asia & Middle East, Rest of World).
   - Your original spreadsheet rates (`Fardar`, `DPS`, `Royal`, `UPS`, `DHL`) are the active foundation for the China & Hong Kong zone.
4. **Real-time Tiered Pricing & Per-Kg Calculation:**
   - Computes: `Per Kg Value for WO = Total Cost ÷ Actual Weight`.
5. **Live Currency Exchange (USD ➔ BDT):**
   - Automatically tracks real-time market exchange rates with one-click refresh and custom monthly bank rate override.
6. **Volumetric Weight (L × W × H / 5000):**
   - Alerts you when carton dimensions exceed actual weight to prevent courier overcharges.
7. **Rate Card Manager:**
   - Edit base rates, fuel surcharges, handling fees for each destination zone with zero Excel formula breakage.
8. **1-Click WO Text Copy & Excel Export:**
   - Formatted for immediate paste into emails, chat, or official download as `.xlsx`.
